Nairobi & Maasai Mara Wildlife Safari

6 Days / 5 Nights • Departs Nairobi • English & Swahili Guides

Maasai Mara Game Reserve & Nairobi Highlights

Experience the jewel of African wildlife with luxury tented camps and professional safari 4x4 land cruisers.

Tour Overview

Embark on an unforgettable African expedition combining the vibrant cosmopolitan energy of Nairobi with the untamed wonder of the Maasai Mara. Witness the Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino) across vast golden savannahs, stay in eco-certified luxury tented lodges, and enjoy authentic cultural encounters with the Maasai community.

Travel Period Open Year-Round
Group Size Max 8 per 4x4 Cruiser
Meals Full Board on Safari

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi & Giraffe Centre

Airport pickup from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO). Transfer to luxury city hotel, visit the Giraffe Centre and enjoy a welcome dinner.

Day 2: Scenic Drive via Great Rift Valley to Maasai Mara

Depart Nairobi descending through the Great Rift Valley viewpoint. Arrive at Mara luxury tented camp for lunch followed by an introductory late afternoon game drive.

Day 3: Full-Day Maasai Mara Game Tracking

Full-day safari expedition with picnic lunch along the Mara River. Track pride of lions, cheetahs, zebras, and seasonal wildebeest herds.

Day 4: Sunrise Safari & Maasai Cultural Experience

Early morning sunrise game drive when predators are most active. Afternoon guided cultural visit to a traditional Maasai Manyatta village.

Day 5: Lake Naivasha Boat Cruise

Depart Mara for scenic Lake Naivasha. Enjoy a guided boat safari spotting hippos and fish eagles, followed by a walking safari on Crescent Island.

Day 6: Nairobi Souvenirs & Departure

Return to Nairobi for local artisan market shopping at Maasai Market, followed by airport transfer for your evening flight home.
